Getting off blocklists

Where to check and request removal from each list. Fix the cause first, or you'll be listed again.

  • Spamhaus Reputation Checker ↗Check IPs and domains on SBL, XBL, CSS, PBL and DBL and request removal.check.spamhaus.org
  • Microsoft (Outlook.com) · Delist ↗Ask Outlook, Hotmail and Live to stop blocking your IP.sender.office.com
  • Barracuda · Removal request ↗Removal request for Barracuda's reputation list.barracudacentral.org
  • SpamCop ↗Check SpamCop's list; entries expire on their own once reports stop.spamcop.net
  • Cloudmark CSI ↗Ask Cloudmark (used by many carriers) to re-evaluate your IP's reputation.csi.cloudmark.com
  • Proofpoint IP Check ↗Check whether Proofpoint blocks your IP and request a review.ipcheck.proofpoint.com
  • Trend Micro ERS ↗Trend Micro IP reputation and removal request.servicecentral.trendmicro.com
  • Abusix Lookup ↗Look up IPs and domains in Abusix Mail Intelligence and request delisting.lookup.abusix.com
  • Vade Sender Tool ↗Vade's sender tool (filter used by Orange, Free and other European carriers).sendertool.vadesecure.com
  • Cisco Talos ↗Cisco IP and domain reputation (IronPort filters).talosintelligence.com
  • Symantec / Broadcom ↗Investigation and removal of IPs blocked by Symantec.ipremoval.sms.symantec.com
  • Sophos IP lookup ↗Check whether Sophos filters block your IP.sophos.com
  • Heatwave DBL (Validity) ↗Domains used for cold email and artificial reputation warming.lookup.validity.tools

Gmail, Microsoft and Yahoo tools

Data from the mailbox providers themselves: reputation, complaints and contact forms.

  • Google Postmaster Tools ↗Requirement compliance, spam rate and delivery issues at Gmail.postmaster.google.com
  • Gmail · Formulario para remitentes ↗To ask Gmail for help when your sends are blocked.support.google.com
  • Microsoft SNDS ↗Data on your IPs at Outlook.com: volume, complaints and spam traps.sendersupport.olc.protection.outlook.com
  • Yahoo Sender Hub ↗Yahoo and AOL requirements, feedback loop and sender dashboard.senders.yahooinc.com
  • Yahoo · Contacto para remitentes ↗Help form when Yahoo blocks or delays your sends.senders.yahooinc.com

Reputation and several lists at once

For an overview of an IP or domain in a few seconds.

  • MultiRBL (valli.org) ↗Check an IP or domain on hundreds of block and allow lists.multirbl.valli.org
  • MxToolbox ↗DNS lookups, blocklists and header analysis.mxtoolbox.com
  • Sender Score (Validity) ↗Reputation score from 0 to 100 for your sending IPs.senderscore.org
  • AbuseIPDB ↗Abuse reports received by an IP (attacks, spam, scans).abuseipdb.com
  • DNSWL ↗Allowlist of legitimate sending IPs; you can apply to be included.dnswl.org

DNS and authentication

To check records, propagation and your SPF, DKIM, DMARC and BIMI setup.

  • Google Admin Toolbox ↗Google's Dig, MX checker and header analyser.toolbox.googleapps.com
  • WhatsMyDNS ↗Check whether a DNS change is already visible worldwide.whatsmydns.net
  • dmarcian Domain Checker ↗Quick SPF, DKIM and DMARC review of a domain.dmarcian.com
  • Learn DMARC ↗Send an email and watch step by step how SPF, DKIM and DMARC are evaluated.learndmarc.com
  • About My Email ↗Send a test email and get a full technical report.aboutmy.email
  • BIMI Group Inspector ↗Check your BIMI record and SVG logo.bimigroup.org
  • DKIM2.eu ↗News, specification and tests for the upcoming DKIM2.dkim2.eu

Inbox and spam tests

To know whether your email lands in the inbox, promotions or spam before sending it to everyone.

  • Mail Tester ↗Spam score for a test email (SpamAssassin, authentication, lists).mail-tester.com
  • GlockApps ↗Inbox placement test across many providers (paid, with a free trial).glockapps.com
  • GMass Inbox Tester ↗Free test: inbox, promotions or spam at Gmail, Outlook and Yahoo.gmass.co
  • Can I Send This ↗Check Google, Yahoo and Outlook sender requirements.canisendthis.com
  • Groups.io · Email Provider Status ↗Real-time delays and bounces at the big providers.groups.io

Email design and HTML

To build emails that look good in every email client.

  • Can I Email ↗Which HTML and CSS works in each email client.caniemail.com
  • Parcel ↗Code editor for emails with previews and checks.parcel.io
  • React Email ↗Components for building emails with React that work in every client.react.email

SMTP error codes

Catalogues of real bounces to understand what each server is telling you.

  • SMTP Field Manual ↗Real SMTP responses from dozens of providers, with their meaning.smtpfieldmanual.com
  • SMTP Codes ↗Dictionary of SMTP codes and enhanced status codes.smtp.codes
  • Yahoo · SMTP error codes ↗Yahoo's errors explained by Yahoo.senders.yahooinc.com
  • Microsoft · NDR ↗Microsoft 365 non-delivery reports and how to fix them.learn.microsoft.com

IPs and networks

Who owns an IP, its history and its location.

  • ARIN Whois / RDAP ↗Official IP owner registry (redirects to each region's registry).search.arin.net
  • ARIN WhoWas ↗History of who held an IP before (requires an ARIN account).arin.net
  • Hurricane Electric BGP ↗Networks (ASN), prefixes and routes for any IP.bgp.he.net
  • MaxMind · GeoIP correction ↗Fix the location of a wrongly geolocated IP.maxmind.com

Learning: blogs and community

The sources I follow to keep up with deliverability.

  • Word to the Wise ↗Laura Atkins's blog: deliverability explained with insight for years.wordtothewise.com
  • Spam Resource ↗Al Iverson's blog, with guides and provider domain lists.spamresource.com
  • Email Geeks ↗The Slack community of email professionals.email.geeks.chat
  • M3AAWG ↗Best practices published by the email industry against abuse.m3aawg.org
  • Email Resources ↗Directory with hundreds of email resources.emailresourc.es
  • r/emaildeliverability ↗Reddit forum to ask deliverability questions.reddit.com

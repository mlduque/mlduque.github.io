BIMI: how to show your logo in the inbox

What BIMI is, what you need for your logo to appear in Gmail, Yahoo and Apple Mail, and how much it costs.

By María Luisa Duque · Email Deliverability Manager · Updated 2026-11-14

In this guide
  1. What BIMI is
  2. Requirements
  3. The record
  4. Is it worth it?

What BIMI is

BIMI (Brand Indicators for Message Identification) lets your brand logo appear next to your emails in the inbox. Besides visibility, it conveys trust: only domains well protected against spoofing can show it. In Gmail, with a certificate, a blue verified checkmark also appears.

Requirements

  1. DMARC in enforcement mode: p=quarantine or p=reject, applied at 100% and without sp=none.
  2. Logo in SVG Tiny PS: a restricted, square SVG format with no scripts or external links, published at an https address.
  3. Certificate: Gmail and Apple Mail require a VMC (Verified Mark Certificate), which needs a registered trademark, or in Gmail also a CMC (Common Mark Certificate), which requires having used the logo for at least a year.
  4. Good reputation: even if you meet everything, providers only show the logo for trusted senders.

The record

It's published as a TXT at default._bimi.yourdomain.com:

v=BIMI1; l=https://yourdomain.com/logo.svg; a=https://yourdomain.com/vmc.pem;

l is the logo address and a the certificate's. Without a certificate, leave a= empty.

Is it worth it?

If you already have DMARC at reject, BIMI is the natural next step and improves brand recognition. If you're still on p=none, focus on protecting your domain first: BIMI is the reward, not the starting point.
